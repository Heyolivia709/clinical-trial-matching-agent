what B1 gets wrong
Criteria disappear rather than resolving to unknown. On EXC-7 it returns a confident narrative conclusion with a citation to a resource that does not exist in the bundle. Cheapest and least auditable.
what B2 recovers
Authored expressions plus timeline tools account for the largest single gain. Coverage becomes complete and aggregation becomes deterministic. Remaining errors are citation errors nobody catches.
what verification adds
A modest F1 gain and a large citation-validity gain. On EXC-7 the first attempt cited a MedicationRequest; the verifier rejected it and one correction produced the administration record. see § 7
