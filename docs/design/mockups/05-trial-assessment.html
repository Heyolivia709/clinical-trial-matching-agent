Reporting status distinct from state
Unknown means evidence is missing, stale, ambiguous, conflicting, unsupported, or unverifiable — a criterion state.
Not assessed means the trial supervisor deliberately skipped the criterion under an early-termination budget — a reporting status, never merged into unknown. Supervisor flags are off in this run, so the count is zero.
