Research prototype · requires qualified human review

These are engineering results on authored synthetic data at small scale. They are not clinical validation, not evidence of clinical safety, and not a claim that the system generalizes to real patient records. Every screening workflow label in this report requires verification through ClinicalTrials.gov and the study team.
